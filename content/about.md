With 20+ years of experience in the tech industry, I've aligned leaders, unblocked developers, and brought fractious teams together to deliver. I'm as comfortable pressure testing an architecture or vendor proposal as I am walking an executive team through a difficult decision.  I've been building reliable, scalable, modular systems most of my career. My industry experience includes aerospace, e-commerce, and especially fintech, where I spent ten years building the back end systems underpinning the sites of some of the biggest names in financial brokerages.

When a platform team lead couldn’t get a critical migration prioritized, I saw why: product managers didn’t understand the risk, so it kept losing to features. I made the risk clear to leadership and secured executive backing. I then led the cross-org engineering effort, resulting in a big bang cutover with zero customer impact.

When a CTO mandated a failure mitigation strategy prior to our first app launch, I recognized that it couldn't be achieved within our constraints. I explained the gap and proposed an alternate strategy, then led the successful cross-org effort to completion, fixing an access control loophole in the process.

When a CPTO needed someone to lead our side of a technical due diligence review by AKF, he picked me to quarterback the event. I received accolades from the reviewers for pulling together the necessary people and materials in such a short time. I made it happen because I knew the architecture from the services up through to the cloud; I knew the key players; and I knew how to get them to share honest assessments of the systems they owned.

Earlier in my career, a vendor gave us a few days’ notice that it would switch the data sources behind the symbology feed all our systems depended on. I insisted on samples before cutover, found significant errors, and escalated to leadership. They pushed the vendor to delay. I then spent three months working with the vendor to fix the feed’s data quality before we accepted the change. I prevented a cutover that would have broken market data on several major brokerage websites.

In short, if you bring me a solution dressed up as a problem, I'll take the time to figure out what you really need. I'll tell you the truth when everyone else is telling you what you want to hear. And then I'll make sure you get what you need.
